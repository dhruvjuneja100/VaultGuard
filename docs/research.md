# VaultGuard Domain Research
VaultGuard belongs to the banking fraud detection domain, addressing the growing problem of loan fraud—where banks in India lose tens of thousands of crores annually due to manipulated financial documents—by using AI-driven document forensics and anomaly detection to automatically verify the authenticity of bank statements.

## What is an NBFC?
An NBFC is a financial institution that provides loans and other financial services like a bank but does not hold a banking license or offer full banking services
## How does a loan application work in India?
A loan application in India involves KYC verification, credit score evaluation, bank statement analysis, and risk assessment before approval. My project improves the bank statement verification step using AI.
## What is KYC?
KYC is the process of verifying a customer’s identity using official documents before providing financial services.
## What is document fraud?
Document fraud means creating, editing, or using fake documents to deceive someone (usually for money, loans, or identity purposes).
## How big is loan fraud in India?
India lost about ₹36,000+ crore in bank frauds in one year (FY 2024–25)
